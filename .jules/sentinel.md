## 2025-02-14 - Enforcing course context and capability checks on redirect endpoints
**Vulnerability:** In `grade.php`, the script accepted a course module ID (`id`) and redirected immediately to `view.php?id=$id` with only a bare `require_login()`, omitting course module verification, course-level login enforcement (`require_login($course, true, $cm)`), and `mod/peerwork:view` capability checks.
**Learning:** Redirect endpoints or trampoline scripts that simply forward to another page often omit the proper course module resolution and context checks, relying entirely on the target page. However, failing to validate access at every entry point violates defense-in-depth and can bypass early permission checks or expose existence of activities across courses.
**Prevention:** Always retrieve `$course` and `$cm` via `get_course_and_cm_from_cmid()`, call `require_login($course, true, $cm)`, and assert appropriate capabilities (`mod/peerwork:view`) before redirecting or performing any operations.
